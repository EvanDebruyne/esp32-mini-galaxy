# Changelog

## 1.0 (2026-10-27)

First public release.

- `ProximitySpheres_SharedHole`: a chunky-pixel Earth on each board; bring the boards together and
  both Earths are torn apart into one shared black hole spanning both screens.
- `ProximitySpheres_Zoom`: a steady 7.5-minute zoom-out from the Earth to the Milky Way, with the
  same merge available at any point.
- Earth map built from Natural Earth coastlines (`sim/make_earth_map.py`).
- Pixel-for-pixel PC simulators of both sketches (`sim/`) and a script that renders the demo
  animations from them (`sim/make_gifs.py`).
- Printable case (`case/`).
- Earlier looks kept in `extras/`: rainbow spheres, a detailed Earth with clouds and city lights,
  one black hole per board, and the original stretch-only spheres.
